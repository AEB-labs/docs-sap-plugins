Thank you for your interest in our Carrier Cloud for SAP. We would like to make it as easy as possible for you to integrate the solution in your SAP processes. Please feel free to contact us if you miss information or if you find a way to make it even easier - just use the "Suggest Edits" function.

Let's start with the business object model.  There is a very good explanation here: [Object Model](https://transport-freight-management.docs.developers.aeb.com/docs/get-to-know-the-object-model)

Carrier Cloud for SAP can be integrated in your processes quite flexible.  We differentiate for the integration scenarios between packing and shipping in SAP S/4HANA + ERP ECC (SD, IM, WM,..)  and especially EWM. Therefore you'll find two different sections in this guide.
