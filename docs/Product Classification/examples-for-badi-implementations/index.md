These examples assist you in handling our data structures. You may even copy & paste them into your development environment. Usually they are executable with only slight modifications (e.g. changing the material number) for debugging purposes.

You need to consider that the data for the transfer might not be in the database. So if a BAdI provides the data you need, do not select it from the database.
